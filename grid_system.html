Class

A class in HTML is an attribute used to give a common name to one or more HTML elements. It is mainly used to apply the same CSS styles or JavaScript functions to multiple elements.

Container

A container is an HTML element used to group and organize related content together. Usually, is used as a container.

Section

A section is an HTML element used to define a thematic grouping of content. It is typically used to divide a webpage into distinct sections, each with its own heading and related content.

Tiger

A tiger is a large and powerful wild animal belonging to the cat family. It is known for its orange coat with black stripes, sharp claws, and strong body. Tigers are carnivorous animals and mainly hunt other animals for food. They are excellent swimmers and can move quietly while hunting. Tigers usually live in forests, grasslands, and wetlands. They play an important role in maintaining the balance of the ecosystem. Today, tigers are threatened by habitat loss and illegal hunting, so protecting them and their natural habitats is very important.

Garden

A garden is a place where plants, flowers, trees, and grass are grown and maintained. Gardens can be found near homes, schools, parks, and other public places. A beautiful garden provides fresh air and makes the surroundings attractive and peaceful. People enjoy spending time in gardens by walking, relaxing, or playing. Gardening is also a good activity because it helps us stay connected with nature. Therefore, we should take care of gardens and protect plants and trees.
